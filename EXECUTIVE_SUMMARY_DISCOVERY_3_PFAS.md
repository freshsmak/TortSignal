## THE SIGNAL

In July 2022, the International Agency for Research on Cancer (IARC) classified **firefighting as a Group 1 carcinogen** - the same classification as tobacco and asbestos. This was based on sufficient evidence for mesothelioma and bladder cancer, with limited evidence for five additional cancer types.

**96% of firefighters have PFAS in their blood** - the highest levels of any occupation.

While AFFF (aqueous film-forming foam) litigation is well-established (MDL 2873, 7,000+ cases), **turnout gear litigation is just beginning** (first lawsuits filed in 2024). This represents a **$25-50 billion second wave** opportunity.

**Key Statistics:**
- 1.1 million firefighters (US): 370K career, 730K volunteer
- IARC Classification: Group 1 (July 2022) - PROVEN carcinogen
- PFAS detection rate: **96%** in blood serum (9,000 tested)
- Cancer elevation: 1.5-2.0x for multiple cancer types
- Regulatory momentum: NFPA banned PFAS in gear (2024), Illinois bans sales (2027)
