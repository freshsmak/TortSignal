## THE REGULATORY DIVERGENCE

**EU banned TiO2 in food (May 2021, effective 2022):**
- Basis: "Cannot rule out genotoxicity after consumption"
- EFSA finding: Nanoparticles accumulate in body, potential DNA damage
- Applied the **precautionary principle**

**US still allows TiO2 (21 CFR 73.575):**
- FDA last reviewed safety **50 years ago** (1966)
- No restriction on nanoparticle content
- Widespread use in candy, gum, processed foods

**This EU/US divergence = litigation opportunity.**
